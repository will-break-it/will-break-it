## The problem

Beach volleyball is organised by word of mouth. Players look for a coach, a training group or a partner at their level, and coaches look for players to fill their sessions. The information sits in chat groups and on court noticeboards.

## What I built

A social network for the sport, started on Berlin's sand courts.

- **Profiles for players.** Over 170,000 player profiles, so that people can find each other.
- **Coaches and sessions.** Players find coaches and their sessions and book them.
- **One codebase, three platforms.** The web app, the iOS app and the Android app are built from a single React Native codebase. A feature ships once and reaches every user.

## Why one codebase

A product run by a small team cannot afford three implementations of the same screen. With one codebase, every hour of work reaches all platforms, and the web version works from a link, with nothing to install first.

## What it shows

As the founder I work across the whole product: the decisions, the app, the backend, the app store releases and the operations. It is the clearest example of what I can ship for a client who needs a product and not just a component.
