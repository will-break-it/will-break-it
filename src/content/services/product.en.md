## What I take on

- **A first version that real users can try.** A working product in weeks, built so that it can grow instead of being thrown away.
- **Websites and booking systems.** Business sites in German and English, with enquiry and booking flows and pages that load fast on a phone.
- **Mobile apps.** React Native for web, iOS and Android from one codebase, or native Swift and Kotlin where the platform matters.
- **Backends.** Supabase and PostgreSQL, authentication, row-level security, edge functions and the data model behind the app.
- **Release and operations.** App Store and Google Play submission, CI/CD, monitoring.
- **Taking over an existing app.** Reading the code, stabilising it and shipping the features that were stuck.

## How I work

I build a rough working version early and put it in front of users. What they do with it decides what gets built next. I use AI agents to move faster on the routine parts and review every result before it ships.

## Where I have done this

- **AlwaysSandy.** My own product: a social network for beach volleyball players and coaches, on web, iOS and Android from one React Native codebase, with over 170,000 player profiles.
- **Dump it!** Voice-first note taking on iOS, Android and web with end-to-end encryption. More than 200 users before I shut it down in 2025.
- **Drip Dropz.** An NFT marketplace on Cardano with more than 10,000 listings and first revenue within 48 hours of launch.
- **CityXcape.** As co-founder and CTO I built the native iOS app and the backend from concept to App Store launch.
- **Underwater Journeys.** Website and booking system for a dive center in Cyprus, built mobile-first.
- **Client websites through Pagebase.** Portfolio, booking and business sites for small companies and freelancers, at a fixed price.

## When I am a good fit

You need a website, a booking system or an app shipped by someone who covers the app, the backend and the release, and who tells you early when a feature costs more than it is worth.
